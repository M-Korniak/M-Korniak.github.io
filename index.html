About

I'm a Master's student at ETH Zurich working on reinforcement learning. I'm interested in when and why replay strategies matter, how to learn representations from action sequences, and how RL post-training can scale the improvement of generalist robot policies.

In 2026 I was a research assistant at ETH Zurich with Max Simchowitz and Andrew Wagenmaker, studying multi-task RL post-training for Vision-Language-Action (VLA) robot policies.
